AI Anomaly Detection Service

Real-time fraud & anomaly detection for Public Sector Social Benefit Programs (Eg., EI, OAS, CPP)

The Anomaly Detection Service (ADS) is a unified, real-time intelligence layer that screens benefit claims across programs the moment they arrive — assigning each a risk score, a plain-language explanation, and a recommended action for investigators.

ADS fuses two complementary signals: a machine-learning model that learns population-level patterns, and a transparent business-rule engine encoding policy and eligibility logic. Every score is explainable — investigators see exactly which indicators drove the assessment and why.

Because ADS spans EI, OAS, and CPP together, it detects what siloed systems miss: the same identity contradicting itself across programs, coordinated identity rings, and abuse patterns hidden inside seasonal volume — in seconds, not months.
